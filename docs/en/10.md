## The idea

A contract describes what a caller can depend on: supported routes, status codes, content type and response shape. Our service has a home response and a health endpoint; an unknown route is a 404. Configuration controls the version field. A contract lets you make changes without silently breaking clients.

## A familiar example

A menu tells customers what they can order and what to expect back.
