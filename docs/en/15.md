## In simple words

Explain why a running app may still be unable to serve users. Start with the short explanation below. Read the new-word meaning, follow one part at a time, and try the activity only when the idea makes sense. You do not need to memorize a list of tool names.

Running means the process has not exited. Healthy means a configured probe has passed. A process can be alive while unable to answer correctly. Probe design should match the purpose: basic liveness is different from readiness to receive traffic.
