## In simple words

Plan access, cost and cleanup before creating cloud resources. Start with the short explanation below. Read the new-word meaning, follow one part at a time, and try the activity only when the idea makes sense. You do not need to memorize a list of tool names.

Cloud adds IAM, networks, compute, storage, TLS, observability and cost ownership. A free-tier label is not a guarantee of zero cost or available capacity. Design before provisioning and distinguish a proposal from tested infrastructure. Our OCI exercise is a worksheet, not an automated deployment.
