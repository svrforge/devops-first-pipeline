## The idea

Combine the pieces into a reviewable demonstration. Add a route with tests, package the service, pass CI, and show a local recovery drill. Evidence should identify the version, command and result. Do not claim production readiness from a local exercise.

## A familiar example

A driving test asks you to demonstrate decisions, not recite every road sign.

## Walk through it

### 1. Build

Add /ready with a documented contract and real tests.

### 2. Verify

Build the image and run the container check.

### 3. Review

Open a focused PR and wait for its required checks.

### 4. Recover

Complete a local failure/recovery timeline with endpoint evidence.
