# Day 1 — What is DevOps? Start with a food-order app.

[All days](README.md) · [Open this lesson](https://svrforge.github.io/devops-first-pipeline/#day=1)

## Today, you will learn to…

Explain what a developer does, what operations does, and why they work together.

**Before you start:** Nothing to install. You only need to know what it means to use an app.

**Tools today:** None

## In simple words

Imagine you own a food-order app. Customers tap a button to order lunch. Someone must build that button, and someone must keep it working. Today, you will understand how those people work together. No coding or installation today.



## Part 1: An app follows instructions

You tap Place order. The computer follows instructions written by a person and shows Order received. Those instructions are called software. The screen you see is only one part of the app.

**New word — Software:** Instructions that tell a computer what to do.

**Example:** Our example app currently charges ₹100 for lunch. Tap the customer button to see what a customer receives.

## Part 2: A developer changes it

You decide to offer a ₹10 discount. A developer changes the app instructions so lunch can cost ₹90. A developer is a person who writes or changes software. Saving this change does not automatically change what customers are using.

**New word — Developer:** A person who writes or changes the instructions of an app.

**Example:** The developer has prepared the discount. Our example customer still sees ₹100 because the changed app has not been made available yet.

## Part 3: Operations makes it available

The change is checked, then the updated app is made available to customers. The operations team helps the app run and stay available. Now a customer can use the discount and pay ₹90. You will learn the technical steps on later days.

**New word — Operations:** The work of running an app and keeping it available for people.

**Example:** The updated example is now available. Tap the customer button: the price has changed from ₹100 to ₹90.

## Part 4: They keep working together

After the update, the team checks whether customers can still order. If something breaks, they investigate and fix it together. DevOps is a way for development and operations to share this responsibility and improve how changes reach users. It is not the name of one tool.

**New word — DevOps:** A way for development and operations to work together to deliver and run software.

**Example:** The discount is useful only if customers can still place an order. Building, checking, making available and looking after the app belong to one shared journey.

## Small activity

```text
No terminal or commands today.
```

## What you should notice

You can explain three things without reading: the developer changes the app; operations helps run it; DevOps helps them work together. You do not need to memorize any tool names.

## Think about this

Imagine the discount appears but the order button stops working. Who should help? Explain why the people building the app and the people running it should investigate together.

## One thing to remember

DevOps connects the people who build software with the people who run it.

## Quick question

After an app update, customers cannot order. What is the DevOps approach?

1. Only the developer should care
2. The people building and running the app investigate together
3. Install more tools without checking the problem

<details><summary>Explain the answer</summary>

They share responsibility for the customer experience. Tools can help later, but teamwork and clear feedback come first.

</details>
